Power Board v4 — Design Review

Project Mains_18V_TEC · Dual-Laser PDH Driver · 3U Eurocard (160 × 100 mm), 2-layer · Review date 2026-06-24

1 · Executive Summary

This is a dual-transformer linear power board for a Eurocard crate: it generates ±22 V auxiliary rails (T1, 50 VA) and a high-current ±6 V TEC supply (T2, 160 VA), distributed through a DIN 41612 Type-M backplane connector. The architecture is sound and the parts are real, current, and in stock on DigiKey. Both high-severity issues are now resolved (2026-06-24): F1 (pass-transistor heatsinking → low-profile Wakefield 657-10ABPE) and F2 (the R32/R33 collector-feed resistors removed and base-stoppers R40–R43 added). Remaining work is medium/low — trace sizing (F4), copper weight (F5), component lifecycle/stock (F6) — plus confirming the low-line headroom note in §4.3.

3 · Power Architecture & Rail Analysis

Mains front-end

120 Vac → PEM01 (TE 7-6609940-4 IEC-C14 with integral 2 A slow-blow fuse + EMI filter + switch) → TH1 (Ametherm SL22 10005, 10 Ω / 5 A NTC inrush limiter) → dual-primary toroids wired for 115 V. Transformers are chassis-mount (off-board), wired in via screw terminals J1–J5. OK — proper inrush limiting, fusing and filtering. Mains net-class clearance is set to 3.0 mm and the on-board mains copper is minimal (PEM is panel-mounted).

Rail 1 — ±22 V auxiliary (T1: Triad VPT30-1670, 50 VA, 2×15 Vac CT)

Bridge D1 (GBU4M, 4 A/1000 V) across the 15-0-15 winding; C3/C4 (2200 µF 50 V) in series, midpoint = GND.
Raw rail ≈ 15 Vac × √2 − Vf ≈ ±20 V
Each series cap sees ≈ 20 V across a 50 V part → OK (2.5× derating).
Pass: Q1 IRF540N / Q2 IRF9540N MOSFET cap-multiplier → ±22VNF → series R8/R9 (4.75 Ω) → ±22 V → J6.P5/P7.
Net rail under load ≈ 18–19 V (label "22 V" is nominal). Adequate to feed downstream ±15 V regulators.

R2/R3 (15 Ω 15 W wirewound) are in parallel with the MOSFET D-S as soft-start/bleed; in steady state Vds≈0 so dissipation ≈ 0 W. OK

Rail 2 — ±6 V TEC supply (T2: Triad VPT18-8800, 160 VA, 2×9 Vac CT) — high current

Bridge D7 (GBU1506, 15 A/600 V, heatsinked) on the 9-0-9 winding, CT = DGND.
Reservoir: 6 × 3300 µF 25 V (C34-39), ≈ 9900 µF per rail.
Raw rail ≈ 9 Vac × √2 − Vf ≈ ±11.5 V (rises to ≈ ±12.5 V at light load, T2 reg = 8%).
120 Hz ripple at 3 A: ΔV = I·t/C = 3 × (1/120) / 9.9 mF ≈ 2.5 Vpp → valley ≈ 9 V.
Pass network: Q3/Q4 MOSFET cap-multiplier + MJL21193 (PNP, −6 V) / MJL21194 (NPN, +6 V) pass pairs,
driven by Q5/Q6, referenced by U11/U12 (LM4051 adj shunt) → ±6V_TEC → J6 power blades.

The 25 V reservoir caps on an ±11.5 V raw rail give 2.2× derating — OK. T2 at 160 VA is generously rated: TEC delivers 2×6 V×3 A = 36 W, and linear losses add a roughly equal amount, so input ≈ 70–80 VA — within rating but note the ~45 % efficiency means the board itself must shed ≈ 36 W of heat.

4 · Thermal Verification

4.1 TEC pass transistors Q7–Q10 (MJL21193/4, TO-264) — RESOLVED (F1)

Update 2026-06-24: heatsink changed from the OMNI-UNI-PF-38 clip (8.07 °C/W) to a Wakefield 657 vertical board-mount sink after confirming the system is fan-cooled and nominal TEC load is ≤ 50 % (≈ 1.5 A/rail). The first pick (657-25ABPE, 63.5 mm tall) was too tall/bulky for the 100×160 mm Eurocard, so the board uses the low-profile 657-10ABPE (25.4 mm tall) — same 41.9×25.4 mm base and pin pattern (shared extrusion 6533), so the composite footprint Heatsinks:TO-264-3_Vertical_HS657-25ABPE is unchanged. At 25.4 mm it matches the existing reservoir-cap height. BOM and schematic/PCB notes updated to match.

Two paralleled pass devices per rail (emitter ballast R34–R37 = 0.1 Ω). Per-device dissipation, V_raw≈11.5 V, V_out=6 V (~6 V drop):

Nominal (≤50%, 1.5 A/rail): P_device = (11.5−6)×1.5 / 2 ≈ 4.5 W
Max (3 A/rail): P_device = (11.5−6)×3 / 2 ≈ 9 W

657-10ABPE (25.4 mm) = 6.8 °C/W natural · 3.7 °C/W @200 LFM ; θ_jc 0.875 + pad 0.7 = 1.575 °C/W ; Ta ≈ 45 °C
Fan ON, nom 4.5 W: θ_total = 1.575 + 3.7 = 5.28 → Tj ≈ 69 °C ✓
Fan ON, max 9 W: θ_total = 5.28 → Tj ≈ 92 °C ✓
Fan FAIL, nom 4.5 W: θ_total = 1.575 + 6.8 = 8.4 → Tj ≈ 83 °C ✓ indefinite
Fan FAIL, max 9 W: θ_total = 8.4 → Tj ≈ 121 °C ✓ survivable (shutdown time)

Verdict: PASS, and it fits the card. With the fan the junction sits ~69 °C at nominal load. A fan failure is survivable indefinitely at nominal load (~83 °C); even the worst case (full 3 A and fan loss simultaneously) only reaches ~121 °C — under the 125 °C target and well under the 150 °C max — with thermal mass giving ample time for an orderly shutdown. 4 × 657-10ABPE (DK 657-10ABPE-ND, in stock, $2.49 ea).

Mechanical follow-ups: 657-10ABPE is 25.4 mm tall (same as the reservoir caps) and bolt-on (adds a #6 screw per device); four fit as 2 rows of 2 (~84×51 mm). Confirm the composite footprint's solder-pin depth against the physical part. If even the 41.9 mm width is too tight, an OMNI-UNI-PF-38 clip (30×27 mm, but 8.07 °C/W and a footprint change) is the ultra-compact fallback. Pass-device dissipation still depends on F2 (now resolved — R32/R33 removed); the numbers above bound the worst case.

F2 detail (resolved 2026-06-24): R32/R33 (4.75 Ω) fed the MJL collector node and, carrying the full load current, capped it at √(2 W/4.75 Ω) ≈ 0.65 A — incompatible with the 3 A spec, and a leftover from the earlier ~16 V raw rail. Working back from F1 (raw ≈ 11.3 V, ripple valley ≈ 8.8 V at 3 A, Vout 6 V, Vce ≥ 2 V): R_max = (8.8 − 6 − 2)/3 ≈ 0.27 Ω — far below 4.75 Ω, and at 0.1 Ω the resistor is lost in the trace/connector parasitics. Resolution: R32/R33 removed (collector tied directly to the cleaned raw node via heavy copper); the pass transistor takes the full ~6 W/device, which the 657-10ABPE covers (§4.1). Because the bases of the paralleled MJL devices were commoned, base-stopper resistors R40–R43 (10 Ω 0805, one per device, placed at the base pin) were added to suppress HF parasitic oscillation. Residual caveat: even with R≈0, the 9 Vac rail gives only ~1.5 V Vce headroom at full 3 A and −10 % line — true worst-case 3 A is at the edge of dropout, so a hard 3 A spec would want a slightly higher TEC secondary tap. Not an issue at the ≤50 % nominal load.
